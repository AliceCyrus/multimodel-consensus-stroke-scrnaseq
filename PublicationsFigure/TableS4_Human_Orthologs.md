## Ortholog Mapping Method

Mouse-to-human ortholog mapping was performed using:
- NCBI HomoloGene database
- Ensembl Compara
- MGI (Mouse Genome Informatics) ortholog annotations

Most mouse genes have 1:1 orthologs with humans. MHC Class II genes (H2-*) map to human HLA genes with functional conservation.
